Do This in the First 5 Minutes

Do

  • Turn the phone off immediately
  • Remove the case, SIM tray, and any accessories
  • Wipe the outside dry with a soft cloth
  • Bring or send it for repair as soon as possible

Don't

  • Don't turn it back on to "check" if it works
  • Don't plug it in to charge
  • Don't put it in rice — it doesn't work and can leave dust in ports
  • Don't use a hairdryer or other direct heat

Should I put my wet phone in rice?

No. Rice does not effectively draw moisture out of a phone, and rice dust can get into ports and speakers. Turn the phone off immediately, do not charge it, and get it to a repair technician as soon as possible instead.

Can a phone be saved after water damage?

Often, yes — especially if it is powered off immediately and brought in quickly. The longer a wet phone stays powered on, the higher the risk of corrosion and permanent damage. We offer a free diagnostic to assess your device before you commit to any repair.
