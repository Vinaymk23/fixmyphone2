Published August 26, 2026 · fixmyPhone

"How much will it cost?" is the first question almost every customer asks us, and it deserves a straight answer instead of "it depends." So here's what phone repairs actually cost in Mysore right now, and — just as importantly — what makes that number go up or down for your specific device.

See the full price list for details on each service.

Why "starting from" instead of a fixed number?

Two things move the price up from the starting rate: your specific phone model, and the part grade you choose.

A flagship phone with a curved OLED display costs more to source parts for than a budget model with a flat LCD screen. And for most repairs you can choose between an original part (matches factory quality exactly, costs more) or a compatible part (a quality third-party alternative, costs less). We'll walk you through both options and their exact price for your model before any work starts — the diagnosis itself is always free.

What actually happens when you book

You tell us your device and the issue — through the quote form, a call, or WhatsApp. We confirm the price and schedule a pick-up time that works for you. Our team collects the device from your doorstep anywhere in Mysore, repairs it at our workshop, and delivers it back — usually the same day for common repairs like screens and batteries.

The bottom line

If a price feels too good to be true, ask what part grade is being used and whether pick-up, delivery, and warranty are actually included — those three things change the real cost of a "cheap" repair fast. We build all three into every price we quote, so what we tell you upfront is what you pay.
